The OpenTelemetry Election Committee is excited to announce the final list of
candidates for the
[2026 OpenTelemetry Governance Committee election](../gc-elections/)!

[Eligible voters][] can help fill four Governance Committee seats through
[Helios Voting][].

- **Voting opens:** 26 October 2026 at 12:00 UTC.
- **Voting closes:** 28 October 2026 at 23:59 [Anywhere on Earth][] (AoE),
  equivalent to 29 October 2026 at 11:59 UTC.

You can find the candidates' pictures, profile links, and descriptions on the
[candidates page][]. Here are their names:

- Antoine Toulme
- Dan Gomez Blanco
- Huxing Zhang
- Josh Suereth
- Juliano Costa
- Kemal Akkoyun
- Matthew Cascio
- Pablo Baeyens Fernández
- Trask Stalnaker
- Vibhanshu Singh

You can check your eligibility by reviewing the [voter roll][]. If you have made
contributions to our ecosystem not measured by the automatic process, you can
[request an exception][] before 23:59 AoE on 23 October 2026 to participate in
the election.
