The OpenTelemetry project maintainers, members of the governance committee, and
technical committee are thrilled to be at [KubeCon NA][] in Salt Lake City from
November 9 - 12, 2026. [Register][] today to join us!

Read on to learn about all the events related to OpenTelemetry during KubeCon.

## OpenTelemetry Contribfest

Join the OpenTelemetry maintainers for the
[OpenTelemetry Contribfest](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/kubecon-cloudnativecon-north-america/program/schedule/?id=1294771)
to make the project better for everyone. You can choose between several
opportunities to contribute, and you can count on maintainers from different
project areas to help you on your first steps. The Contribfest takes place on
Tuesday, November 10 • 11:30 - 12:50.

## Maintainer Summit

Held on November 8, one day prior to the co-located events and two days prior to
the conference, the Maintainer Summit is an exclusive opportunity for the people
behind the project to gather face-to-face. [Members][membership] of the
OpenTelemetry organization will come together to collaborate, learn, share
ideas, solve problems, and enrich their projects. See the [full
schedule][summit schedule] for all the details. [Sign up][maintainer summit]
today!

> [!IMPORTANT] Important access note
>
> You must be registered for KubeCon + CloudNativeCon to access the **Maintainer
> Summit**. You also must be a member of a CNCF incubating project, such as
> OpenTelemetry. Maintainer Summit requires a separate registration. For
> eligibility requirements and to register, see [Maintainer Summit][].

## Observability Day

[Observability Day][] fosters collaboration, discussion, and knowledge sharing
with a focus on cloud native observability projects. This event will be held on
November 9, 2026, from 9:00 to 17:30. Look for your favorite talks about
observability and OpenTelemetry in the [schedule][obs-day-sched].

> [!IMPORTANT] Important access note
>
> You need an _all-access_ pass to attend **Observability Day**. For details,
> see [KubeCon registration][register].

## OpenTelemetry at the Project Pavilion

Come meet the OpenTelemetry community at the Project Pavilion on Thursday,
November 12, from 10:00 to 14:00! Stop by for an informal chat, ask questions,
share your experiences, or find out how to get involved in the project.

## OpenTelemetry Community Awards

OpenTelemetry is a community-driven project, fueled by a group of awesome humans
who are actively revolutionizing the field of observability with their
contributions. Whether it’s through code, documentation, project management,
outreach, adoption, or simply helping others, we want to recognize these
contributions and the people behind them.

Nominations for this year's awards are [open now](/blog/2026/community-awards/),
and we'll announce the winners during KubeCon + CloudNativeCon North America!

Come join us to listen, learn, and get involved in OpenTelemetry.

See you in Salt Lake City!
